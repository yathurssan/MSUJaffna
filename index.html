Welcome

The Medical Students' Union is the elected body that represents every undergraduate of the Faculty of Medicine, University of Jaffna.

We speak for students on academic matters, welfare and hostel facilities, and work alongside the Faculty administration and the Teaching Hospital, Jaffna. The Union also coordinates the clubs and societies of the Faculty, and organises health camps, blood donation drives and awareness programmes across the Northern Province.

If you have a concern about your studies, your welfare or a Faculty facility, speak to your batch representative or write to the Union directly. Every message is read by the committee.
